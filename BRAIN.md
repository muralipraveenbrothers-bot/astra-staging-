# SHASTRA AI — BRAIN & INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE

## 1. 13 Clinical Thinking Modes

All modes operate as specialized lenses of the unified SHASTRA Brain:

1. **General Copilot**: Balanced operations, clinical summaries, and patient status.
2. **Communication Coach**: Calibrated bedside communication advice.
3. **Patient Psychology**: Anxiety reduction, psychological safety, and compliance reinforcement.
4. **Attendant Dynamics**: Managing family expectations and clear prognosis framing.
5. **Clinical Roleplay**: Case simulation and medical team preparation.
6. **Senior Consultant**: Highly rigorous, conservative second opinions with guideline checks.
7. **Patient/Family Explanation**: Plain-language medical translation in English, Telugu, Hindi, Tamil.
8. **Diagnostic Reasoning**: Multi-system differential diagnosis with must-not-miss red flags.
9. **Cellular Pathophysiology**: Deep molecular, organ-level, and temporal trajectory modeling.
10. **Evidence & Literature**: Grounded retrieval from PubMed/ClinicalTrials with Tiers A–V.
11. **Safety & Blindspot Detection**: Drug-drug interactions, renal/hepatic dosing, and allergy alerts.
12. **Medical Image Intelligence**: Radiology assistance, measurement verification, and structured reporting.
13. **Fast Lookup**: Low-latency clinical facts and standard dosages.

## 2. Sparse Data Reasoning Calibration

When clinical data is incomplete, SHASTRA does not hallucinate certainty. It returns:
- **Most Likely Conditions**
- **Must-Not-Miss Red Flags**
- **Missing Information Required**
- **Highest-Value Next Questions**
- **Highest-Value Next Investigations**
- **Immediate Safety Considerations**
